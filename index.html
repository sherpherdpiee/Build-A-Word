How to play with your hand
  1. Stand or sit where the webcam can see one hand clearly.

  4. Clench a fist to strike the locked target with a lightning bolt.

Camera access normally requires HTTPS or localhost and explicit permission; an HTML file opened directly from disk may not allow webcam access in every browser. If webcam access or hand tracking is unavailable, all game questions remain playable with mouse, touch and keyboard. Teacher note: The microphone feature uses your browser’s speech-recognition technology to listen for the target word. It checks recognised words, not perfect pronunciation. Microphone recognition usually requires internet access and a supported browser (often Chrome or Edge), and the page should be opened with microphone permission. If voice recognition is unavailable, pupils can still play all other parts of the game and the teacher can listen to their reading.
